Dudi

A private personal assistant for your own life data.

Dudi is an iPhone app with a personal server that each person runs for themselves. It brings together what you choose to connect (your calendar, email, contacts, health data, notes and chats) so you can ask questions about your own life, keep a notebook of tasks and decisions, and get reminders.

Your data stays on your own server, encrypted. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models.
