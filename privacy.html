Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who processes your data

LifeOS runs on a personal server operated by or for the person who uses it. There is no central LifeOS service: your data is stored only on that server, encrypted at rest, and reached only over a private network.

What LifeOS reads

Only the sources you connect and switch on in the app. With your Google account, and only with your consent on Google's sign-in page:

  • Gmail (read-only): sender, subject, date and labels of your email; the text only if you turn it on.
  • Google Calendar: your events (title, time, place, description, attendee names), and changes you ask for: LifeOS creates, moves or deletes an event only after you confirm it in the app.
  • Google Contacts (read-only): name, company, job title, birthday and email addresses. Phone numbers are not kept.
  • Google Drive (read-only): file names and, if you turn it on, the text of documents.

How it is used

To answer your own questions, show your timeline, build your notebook and people cards, and remind you. When you ask a question, the parts of your data that match it may be sent to the AI provider that writes the answer (Claude by Anthropic), after email addresses and phone numbers are removed. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.

Google user data: Limited Use

LifeOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is never sold, never used for advertising, never transferred to others except as described above to answer your questions, and never read by people except with your explicit permission or as required by law.

Keeping and deleting

Data is kept until you delete it. In the app you can turn any source off, export your data, or delete it. You can also remove LifeOS's access at any time in your Google Account under Security, Third-party access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the person who runs your LifeOS server.
